Assumptions

Data provenance. Aquarius pool TVL, 24h volume and APY read from amm-api.aqua.network/pools/ on 12 Aug 2026 (values are 1e7-scaled in the API and converted here). Boosted APR figures and the volatile/stable rows are read from the Aquarius UI the same day. Blend borrow APY, emissions and liability factors are read from the Blend pool UI (market size $189.96M) on 12 Aug 2026, and independently reconstructed from the pool contract CAJJZSGMMM3PD7N33TAPHGBUGTB43OC73HVIK2L2G6BNGGGYOSSYBXBD via get_reserve - which reproduces the UI's 536.60M available and 0.10% rate exactly. Prices: XLM $0.161989, AQUA $0.00032645 (CoinGecko).

Method. net APY = LP APY × L − net borrow APR × (L−1). Max leverage = 1/(1 − collateral factor / liability factor), the Blend liquidation boundary; the slider is capped at 92% of it. Borrow-rate projection uses Blend's three-slope reactive model (rate = curve(utilisation) x ir_mod) at the current modifier. Dilution assumes emissions and trading volume are fixed, so APY scales by TVL/(TVL + new exposure) — a first-order approximation, not a pool simulation.

Not investment advice. Outputs are arithmetic consequences of the inputs shown, not forecasts. Blend borrow rates are utilisation-driven and move without notice; a rate above the LP APY makes a leveraged position lose money. Leveraged LP positions can be liquidated and can lose more than an unleveraged position.
